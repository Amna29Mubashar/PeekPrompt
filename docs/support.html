Frequently asked questions

Where are the controls? I don't see a Dock icon.

PeekPrompt lives in your menu bar and on the prompter itself. Hover over the prompter to show its controls, or right-click it for a menu with Settings and Quit. On crowded MacBook menu bars, macOS may hide the menu bar icon behind the notch.

Does it show up when I share my screen?

No. With "Hide prompter from screenshots, screen sharing and recordings" turned on (the default), it stays out of Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, QuickTime and screenshots. On macOS 15.4 and later, some recorders, including Loom's full-screen mode, can still capture it, and Apple doesn't let apps prevent that. In Loom, record "Camera only" or a single window instead.

Voice-Activated mode doesn't move, or moves when I'm quiet.

Open Settings → Scrolling and watch the mic bar while you talk. It should cross the white line when you speak but not in silence. Adjust Sensitivity until it does.

Word Tracking isn't following me.

Make sure speech recognition is allowed in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition, and that your Mac's language matches the language of your script.

How do I show one slide at a time?

Turn on Presentation mode in Settings → Scrolling. Import a PowerPoint (its speaker notes become slides), or separate slides with a line containing just ---. Use ⌃⌥→ and ⌃⌥← to change slides.

The phone remote won't connect.

Your phone and Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi network, and you need to allow PeekPrompt to "find devices on your local network" when macOS asks. Each time the remote is turned on it makes a new link, so scan the latest QR code.

Magic Polish says "Basic cleanup".

AI rewriting needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings. Without it, PeekPrompt does a simpler cleanup on your Mac.
