Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: PeekPrompt doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your scripts stay on your Mac.

Your scripts and settings

Scripts, folders and settings are saved only on your Mac, inside PeekPrompt's own private storage. They are never uploaded. Files you import are read once to copy their text into a script. PeekPrompt only opens files you choose.

Microphone

PeekPrompt uses the microphone, with your permission, to scroll while you speak (Voice-Activated mode) and to make the border glow. For these features it only measures how loud the sound is, on your Mac. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere by PeekPrompt.

Speech recognition (Word Tracking, Auto speed and Dictation)

These optional features use Apple's built-in speech recognition, with your permission. On Macs that support on-device recognition, PeekPrompt asks for it to run entirely on your Mac. On other Macs, Apple may process the audio on its servers to turn it into text. That processing is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. PeekPrompt receives only the resulting text, uses it to follow along or to type your script, and never stores or sends it anywhere.

Magic Polish

Magic Polish uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model when it's available, so your script stays on your Mac. When it isn't available, a simple built-in cleanup runs on your Mac instead.

Phone remote

If you turn on the phone remote, PeekPrompt runs a small web page on your local network so a phone on the same Wi-Fi can control it. It's off by default, never connects to the internet, and requires a secret code that changes each time you turn it on. It only sends the prompter's status (for example, whether it's playing and the script's title) to the phone you connected.

Siri and Shortcuts

PeekPrompt offers actions like "Start PeekPrompt" to Siri and the Shortcuts app. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. PeekPrompt doesn't receive any information from them beyond the action itself.

Children

PeekPrompt doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
